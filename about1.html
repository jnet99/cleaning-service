Our story

Born from family, built on trust

Limpieza y Brillo was founded by Citlali N. and Betty C. — a mother and daughter team who shared a simple dream: to build something of their own while bringing real care and professionalism into people's homes.

Citlali, the mom, brought decades of experience keeping a spotless household and the kind of attention to detail that only a parent can have. Betty, her daughter, brought the drive, energy, and business sense to turn that vision into a real company. Together, they make an unstoppable team.

What started as word-of-mouth referrals in their neighborhood has grown into a trusted cleaning service serving families across the Dallas area. Through every job, big or small, they've stayed true to what got them started: treating every home like it's their own.

When you hire Limpieza y Brillo, you're not hiring a faceless company — you're welcoming family into your home.
